Bio

Hello, my name is Aniwene Madolomani. 

**Anivo Project**
For a long time I have desired to do this project. At first, it was not called Anivo, and if you ask me, i wouldnt know the day the name Anivo was birthed. But Simply Anivo means, Aniwene's devotionals. I have been fascinated with devotionals created, and the ones I have been following up on and always desired to create devotionals that would fit me.

Challenges:
The devotionals i had been doing had the following challenges.
- They were created monthly/yearly (This means, you become dependent on the author/pastor every year for a new devotional)
- They did not cover the whole bible (devotionals usually just take one or two verses and then explain that, for a Bible scholar, i thought it would be hindering)
- They based on the author's intepretation and understanding (When its always the Author speaking, the reader has no opinion, they become dumb and numb, and their point of reference becomes what the author said and not what they understood from the passage)
- They were not timeless (Some devotionals, were too based on the current world situations that you can not repeat the same devotionals many years to come. Or because there are new devotionals being released then you now have to focus on new ones instead of old ones)
- They kept confusing me with Bible Versions (Someday they would use this Bible version, someday they would use another bible version to fit the new intepretation which makes it confusing for me to choose a version to focus on.)

I understand that these may not be the same experiences to everyone, however, to me, they were a big issue. Enough to put me on a path to look for a way to create Aniwene Devotionals (Anivo). Special devotionals designed for me, to fix the issues that I was facing in previous devotionals. 

As i started doing research, trying to compare if other people are facing the same issues, i realized most people don't study the bible at all! Others do devotionals daily, but don't even remember what they studied. That is when i felt in my spirit that Anivo could be more. It could do more. And it can touch more people than just Aniwene (me). It went from being a personal project, to a global focused project. Hence Anivo Project.

Focus and Methods:
Here is the direction i am going with currently in Anivo Project, and the reasons behind each.
- Anivo Version Bible: The introduction of Anivo Version Bible solves the problem of Bible version i had. With Anivo Versions, millions of people can take part and contribute ideas and help create a version that is not only understandable, but also trusted and based on TRUTH.
- KJV, ASV, WEBB, WEBBE, WEBU & Original Texts: Anivo Version Bibles will be translated straight from the mentioned versions of the Bible, after being compared to the original texts and languages for context and reference. All of them are trusted BIBLES but they are also in the Public Domain, meaning we can use them to translate, create new versions and more. 
- Github: To Ensure that all the progresses and changes are seen, we need to use github as our resource. This will ensure no text is changed behind the scenes, behind the eyes of believers and behind the council of God on earth (Which is the Church of Christ - Here i mean every true believer not a specific church or denomination. Those who have truly given their lives to Jesus, and made him their Lord and savior.)
- Innovation: Anivo Project is to be a backbone, a fascilitator, allowing Christian innovators to explore the Bible and Gospel, share it with others, earn from their work and more. (Eg, we will enable APIs or other features so developers can use Anivo Project for their online bible sites, apps, and more)
- Controlled Decentralization: The control here is purely based on Biblical Context. Every project has to align with what the Bible is instructing us. And, we also need to allow decentralization and freedom, eg later maybe create our own blockchain so Christian can benefit from the new direction of Crypto. We can have smart contracts, so people doing projects can be paid, contributors in the project can earn and more. We are THE chosen ones, we are beneficiaries of new innovations and technology. 
- Gospel, Tech & Wealth: These are our three areas of Focus.

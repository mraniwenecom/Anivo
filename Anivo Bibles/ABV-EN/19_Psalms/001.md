# Psalms 1

**1** Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly, nor standeth in the way of sinners, nor sitteth in the seat of the scornful.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the wicked, Nor standeth in the way of sinners, Nor sitteth in the seat of scoffers:

> **WEB:** Blessed is the man who doesn’t walk in the counsel of the wicked,
> 
> **WEBBE:** Blessed is the man who doesn’t walk in the counsel of the wicked,
> 
> **WEBU:** Blessed is the man who doesn’t walk in the counsel of the wicked,

**2** But his delight is in the law of the LORD; and in his law doth he meditate day and night.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
>

> **WEB:** but his delight is in Yahweh’s law.
> 
> **WEBBE:** but his delight is in the LORD’s law.
> 
> **WEBU:** but his delight is in the LORD’s law.

**3** And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season; his leaf also shall not wither; and whatsoever he doeth shall prosper.

> 
> **WEBBE:** He will be like a tree planted by the streams of water,
> 
> **WEBU:** He will be like a tree planted by the streams of water,

**4** The ungodly are not so: but are like the chaff which the wind driveth away.

> **WEBU:** The wicked are not so,

**5** Therefore the ungodly shall not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.

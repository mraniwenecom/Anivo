# Psalms 125

**1** They that trust in the LORD shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed, but abideth for ever.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** They that trust in Jehovah Are as mount Zion, which cannot be moved, but abideth for ever.
> 
> **WEB:** Those who trust in Yahweh are as Mount Zion,
> 
> **WEBBE:** Those who trust in the LORD are as Mount Zion,
> 
> **WEBU:** Those who trust in the LORD are as Mount Zion,

**2** As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people from henceforth even for ever.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, So Jehovah is round about his people From this time forth and for evermore.
> 
> **WEB:** As the mountains surround Jerusalem,
> 
> **WEBBE:** As the mountains surround Jerusalem,
> 
> **WEBU:** As the mountains surround Jerusalem,

**3** For the rod of the wicked shall not rest upon the lot of the righteous; lest the righteous put forth their hands unto iniquity.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** For the sceptre of wickedness shall not rest upon the lot of the righteous; That the righteous put not forth their hands unto iniquity.
> 
> **WEB:** For the scepter of wickedness won’t remain over the allotment of the righteous,
> 
> **WEBBE:** For the sceptre of wickedness won’t remain over the allotment of the righteous,
> 
> **WEBU:** For the scepter of wickedness won’t remain over the allotment of the righteous,

**4** Do good, O LORD, unto those that be good, and to them that are upright in their hearts.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** Do good, O Jehovah, unto those that are good, And to them that are upright in their hearts.
> 
> **WEB:** Do good, Yahweh, to those who are good,
> 
> **WEBBE:** Do good, LORD, to those who are good,
> 
> **WEBU:** Do good, LORD, to those who are good,

**5** As for such as turn aside unto their crooked ways, the LORD shall lead them forth with the workers of iniquity: but peace shall be upon Israel.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** But as for such as turn aside unto their crooked ways, Jehovah will lead them forth with the workers of iniquity. Peace be upon Israel.
> 
> **WEB:** But as for those who turn away to their crooked ways,
> 
> **WEBBE:** But as for those who turn away to their crooked ways,
> 
> **WEBU:** But as for those who turn away to their crooked ways,

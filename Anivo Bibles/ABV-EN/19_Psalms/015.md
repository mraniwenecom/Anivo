# Psalms 15

**1** Lord, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill?

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** Jehovah, who shall sojourn in thy tabernacle? Who shall dwell in thy holy hill?
> 
> **WEB:** Yahweh, who shall dwell in your sanctuary?
> 
> **WEBBE:** LORD, who shall dwell in your sanctuary?
> 
> **WEBU:** LORD, who shall dwell in your sanctuary?

**2** He that walketh uprightly, and worketh righteousness, and speaketh the truth in his heart.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** He that walketh uprightly, and worketh righteousness, And speaketh truth in his heart;
> 
> **WEB:** He who walks blamelessly and does what is right,
> 
> **WEBBE:** He who walks blamelessly and does what is right,
> 
> **WEBU:** He who walks blamelessly and does what is right,

**3** He that backbiteth not with his tongue, nor doeth evil to his neighbour, nor taketh up a reproach against his neighbour.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** He that slandereth not with his tongue, Nor doeth evil to his friend, Nor taketh up a reproach against his neighbor;
> 
> **WEB:** he who doesn’t slander with his tongue,
> 
> **WEBBE:** he who doesn’t slander with his tongue,
> 
> **WEBU:** he who doesn’t slander with his tongue,

**4** In whose eyes a vile person is contemned; but he honoureth them that fear the LORD. He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** In whose eyes a reprobate is despised, But who honoreth them that fear Jehovah; He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not;
> 
> **WEB:** in whose eyes a vile man is despised,
> 
> **WEBBE:** in whose eyes a vile man is despised,
> 
> **WEBU:** in whose eyes a vile man is despised,

**5** He that putteth not out his money to usury, nor taketh reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** He that putteth not out his money to interest, Nor taketh reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.
> 
> **WEB:** he who doesn’t lend out his money for usury,
> 
> **WEBBE:** he who doesn’t lend out his money for usury,
> 
> **WEBU:** he who doesn’t lend out his money for usury,

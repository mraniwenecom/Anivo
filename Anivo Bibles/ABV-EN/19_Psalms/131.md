# Psalms 131

**1** Lord, my heart is not haughty, nor mine eyes lofty: neither do I exercise myself in great matters, or in things too high for me.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** Jehovah, my heart is not haughty, nor mine eyes lofty; Neither do I exercise myself in great matters, Or in things too wonderful for me.
> 
> **WEB:** Yahweh, my heart isn’t arrogant, nor my eyes lofty;
> 
> **WEBBE:** LORD, my heart isn’t arrogant, nor my eyes lofty;
> 
> **WEBU:** LORD, my heart isn’t arrogant, nor my eyes lofty;

**2** Surely I have behaved and quieted myself, as a child that is weaned of his mother: my soul is even as a weaned child.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul; Like a weaned child with his mother, Like a weaned child is my soul within me.
> 
> **WEB:** Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul,
> 
> **WEBBE:** Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul,
> 
> **WEBU:** Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul,

**3** Let Israel hope in the LORD from henceforth and for ever.

> **Translation note:** Not yet translated.
> 
> **ASV:** O Israel, hope in Jehovah From this time forth and for evermore.
> 
> **WEB:** Israel, hope in Yahweh,
> 
> **WEBBE:** Israel, hope in the LORD,
> 
> **WEBU:** Israel, hope in the LORD,
